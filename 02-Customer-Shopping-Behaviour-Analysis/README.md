#Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behaviour to understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and buying trends.

The project uses customer purchase data to examine how factors such as age, gender, product category, location, and product color influence shopping patterns.

Tableau Public was used to analyze the dataset and create visualizations to identify important customer purchasing trends and generate meaningful business insights.

2. Problem Statement

Understanding customer shopping behaviour is essential for businesses to improve their marketing strategies, customer satisfaction, and sales performance.

However, businesses need to identify which customer groups purchase more frequently, which product categories are preferred, and which locations and product colors attract more customers.

This project analyzes customer shopping data to identify these patterns and provide useful insights that can support better business decision-making.

3. Project Objectives

- To analyze customer purchases across different age groups.
- To compare purchasing patterns between different genders.
- To identify the most purchased product categories.
- To determine the locations with the highest purchase counts.
- To identify the most preferred product colors.
- To generate meaningful insights and business recommendations through data visualization.

4. Dataset Description

The project uses a Customer Shopping Behaviour dataset containing customer-related and purchase-related information.

The dataset includes the following attributes:

- Customer ID
- Age
- Gender
- Category
- Location
- Color

The dataset was used to analyze customer purchasing patterns and identify trends across different customer segments.

5. Tools and Technologies Used

- Tableau Public: Used for data visualization, analysis, and dashboard creation.
- Microsoft Excel: Used for data understanding and preparation.
- GitHub: Used for project documentation and portfolio presentation.

6. Data Analysis

The dataset was analyzed using Tableau Public. Five different visualizations were created to understand customer shopping behaviour.

Analysis Performed

1. Age-wise Purchase Count: Analyzed purchases across different age groups.
2. Gender-wise Purchase Count: Compared purchases among male, female, and other customers.
3. Category-wise Purchase Count: Analyzed purchasing patterns across different product categories.
4. Location-wise Purchase Count: Identified locations with higher purchase counts.
5. Color-wise Purchase Count: Analyzed customer preferences for different product colors.

Analysis Focus

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

1. Which age group has the highest purchase count?
2. How do purchasing patterns differ by gender?
3. Which product category receives the most purchases?
4. Which location has the highest purchase count?
5. Which product colors are most popular?

7. Key Findings

Based on the analysis and Tableau visualizations, the following findings were identified:

7.1 Age-wise Purchase Analysis

- Customers aged 41 recorded the highest purchase count.
- The purchase count for age 41 was 114 purchases.

Insight: This indicates that customers aged 41 represent the highest purchasing age group in the analyzed data.

7.2 Gender-wise Purchase Analysis

The gender-wise analysis identified the following purchase counts:

Gender| Purchase Count
Male| 3,018
Female| 1,670
Other| 362

Insight: Male customers recorded the highest purchase count, followed by female customers and other customers.

7.3 Category-wise Purchase Analysis

The category-wise analysis identified the following purchase counts:

Product Category| Purchase Count
Clothing| 48,419
Accessories| 37,385
Footwear| 15,256
Outerwear| 8,173
Electronics| 4,316

Insight: Clothing recorded the highest purchase count, followed by Accessories. Electronics recorded the lowest purchase count among the listed categories.

7.4 Location-wise Purchase Analysis

- North Carolina recorded the highest purchase count.
- Total purchase count: 337.

Insight: North Carolina was the leading location in terms of purchase count in the analyzed dataset.

7.5 Color-wise Purchase Analysis

The analysis identified the following five leading product colors:

Product Color| Purchase Count
Beige| 404
Black| 387
Silver| 367
Olive| 366
White| 365

Insight: Beige was the most preferred color among the five leading colors, with 404 purchases, followed by Black with 387 purchases.

8. Recommendations

Based on the findings of the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested for businesses:

1. Age-based Marketing: Businesses can focus on customers around the age group of 41, which recorded the highest purchase count, and develop targeted marketing campaigns.

2. Gender-based Marketing: Since male customers recorded higher purchases, businesses can study their preferences while also developing strategies to increase engagement among female and other customer segments.

3. Product Category Promotion: Businesses should focus on popular categories such as Clothing and Accessories by maintaining product availability and developing attractive promotional offers.

4. Location-based Marketing: Businesses can strengthen their marketing activities in North Carolina, which recorded the highest purchase count, while exploring opportunities in other locations.

5. Color-based Inventory Planning: Businesses can maintain sufficient inventory of popular colors such as Beige and Black based on the observed purchase counts.

These recommendations can help businesses understand customer preferences, improve marketing strategies, and make more informed business decisions.

10. Conclusion

The Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis project provided useful insights into customer purchasing patterns and preferences across different age groups, genders, product categories, locations, and product colors.

The analysis identified age 41 as the highest purchasing age group, male customers as the leading gender segment, Clothing as the most purchased category, North Carolina as the leading location, and Beige as the most purchased color among the five leading colors.

The findings and recommendations can help businesses better understand their customers, improve marketing strategies, plan inventory, and make data-driven decisions.
