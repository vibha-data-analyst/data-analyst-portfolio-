# E-commerce Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing an E-commerce Sales Dataset using Tableau to understand sales performance, product popularity, and customer purchasing patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
- Understand monthly revenue trends.
- Analyze country-wise sales performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Tableau – Data Visualization and Dashboard Creation.

## Dataset Information
The project uses an E-commerce Sales Dataset to analyze orders, products, countries, and revenue trends.

## Key Findings
- Total orders analyzed: 22,062.
- Country-wise sales performance.
- Top 10 best-selling products.
- Monthly revenue trends.

## Solutions & Recommendations
- Focus on best-selling products.
- Improve sales performance across countries.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends.

## Project Impact
- Better understanding of sales performance.
- Improved business decision-making.
- Better sales and revenue planning.
